    Features

    Everything the farm needs.
    Nothing it doesn't.

    Purpose-built tools for poultry operations — designed to be as hardworking as the people using them.

    Flock Management

    Track batches, breeds, and growth stages with complete health histories for every bird in every pen.

    Inventory Control

    Feed, medicine, and supplies with live stock counts and automatic low-stock alerts before anything runs out.

    Egg Production

    Daily collection logs, grading, and production trends — so no egg goes uncounted and none unexplained.

    Sales & Revenue

    Record every sale and watch revenue and profit margins update in real time across all product lines.

    Reports & Analytics

    One-click reports with charts, trends, and Excel export for any date range you need.

    Smart Alerts

    Vaccination schedules, expiring medicine, and shrinking stock — flagged before they become problems.

    From coop to dashboard

    03
