How order requests work

The same five steps for every request.

  • You submit a request with the product and quantity you need.
  • The farm reviews stock and sends back a quoted price.
  • You accept or decline the quote — quotes lapse if left too long.
  • On acceptance the farm prepares the order and records the sale against your account.
  • You can cancel any time before the quote is accepted.
