How deliveries work

Two directions, one queue.

  • Supplier announced — you tell the farm what is coming. You can cancel your own announcement while it is still pending.
  • Farm ordered — the farm raises a purchase order. Confirm or adjust the date above; the farm is notified either way. Once you answer it leaves your waiting list and stays Confirmed (still adjustable) until the farm books it.
  • Once the goods physically arrive, farm staff mark the notice Received, which records the expense against your account.
  • Received deliveries appear in Purchase History.
